Project Overview

A Study on Capital Utilisation Efficiency and Financial Performance at Rane (Madras) Limited, Varanavasi.

Objectives

  • Assess how effectively capital is utilised.
  • Evaluate overall financial performance.
  • Understand liquidity, funding and profitability positions.

Financial Analysis Methods

  • Financial performance and ratio analysis
  • Working capital analysis
  • Comparative and common-size statements
  • Trend analysis
  • Cash flow and fund flow analysis

Key Findings

  • Improved asset utilisation.
  • Weak liquidity position.
  • Negative working capital.
  • High dependence on borrowings.
  • Unstable profitability due to increasing expenses.

Business Implications

Efficient use of assets is a strength, but weak liquidity and reliance on borrowings suggest the need for tighter working capital management and expense control.
